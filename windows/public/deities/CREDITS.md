# Deity art credits — `windows/public/deities/`

All art in this folder is display art shown with reverence (welcome hero,
greeting blessing, festival art). None of it is wired to any poke, drag, or
reaction handler, and none of it depicts the companion Narayan.

## Round 1 — original art made for Bholeynath

- `shiva-hero.jpg` — Shiva in meditation on Mount Kailash, created for this project.
- `ganesha.jpg` — Ganesha with modaks, created for this project.
- `durga.jpg` — Durga on her lion, created for this project.

## Round 2 — public-domain paintings from Wikimedia Commons

These are faithful reproductions of two-dimensional public-domain works of
art; the reproductions are likewise public domain (Wikimedia Commons PD-Art).
Optimized copies only are shipped here — the full-resolution originals are
not in the repo tree.

- `lakshmi.jpg` — Raja Ravi Varma (1848–1906), *Goddess Lakshmi*, oil on
  canvas. Commons file: `File:Ravi_Varma-Lakshmi.jpg`.
- `saraswati.jpg` — Raja Ravi Varma (1848–1906), *Goddess Saraswati*, 1896,
  oil on canvas (Maharaja Fateh Singh Museum, Vadodara). Commons file:
  `File:Saraswati.jpg`.
- `krishna.jpg` — Miskin, *Krishna Holds Up Mount Govardhan to Shelter the
  Villagers of Braj*, c. 1590–95, ink, opaque watercolor and gold on paper
  (Metropolitan Museum of Art, CC0). Commons file:
  `File:Krishna_Holds_Up_Mount_Govardhan_to_Shelter_the_Villagers_of_Braj,_ca.1590–95,_The_Met.jpg`.
- `hanuman.jpg` — Anonymous, *Hanuman carrying Rama on his shoulders*,
  Malwa, mid-17th century, opaque watercolor on paper (National Museum, New
  Delhi). Commons file: `File:Hanuman_carrying_Rama_on_his_shoulders.jpg`.
